ARKA SMP CORE - PLAYER COMMANDS
===============================
No permission needed. Everything also works by tapping menus (no typing), good for mobile.

MENU
----
/arka  or  /arka menu              Main menu: homes, spawn, teleport requests, random teleport, claims

HOMES
-----
/homes                             Open your homes menu (tap a home to teleport, tap an empty slot to set one)
/home                              Same menu
/home <name>                       Teleport to a home
/sethome [name]                    Set a home here (default name: home)
/delhome [name]                    Delete a home (no name = delete mode menu)
  Names: letters, numbers, - and _ (max 16)

SPAWN
-----
/spawn                             Teleport to spawn

TELEPORT REQUESTS (TPA)
-----------------------
/tpa [player]                      Ask to teleport to a player (no name = pick from a menu)
/tpahere [player]                  Ask a player to teleport to you
/tpaccept [player]                 Accept a request        (alias: /tpyes)
/tpdeny [player]                   Deny a request          (alias: /tpno)
/tpcancel                          Cancel your sent requests
  Requests expire after 60 seconds. Accept/Deny buttons also appear in chat.

RANDOM TELEPORT
---------------
/rtp                               Teleport to a random safe spot    (aliases: /wild, /randomtp)

AFK
---
/afk                               Toggle AFK. You earn no play time while AFK, and cannot be attacked by players.

LAND CLAIMS
-----------
/claim                             Open the claims menu          (aliases: /landclaim, /lc)
/claim tool                        Get the claim tool (golden shovel)
/claim blocks                      Show your claim blocks (left / total / used)
/claim info                        Info about the claim you stand in
/claim list                        List your claims
/claim show                        Show borders of the claim you stand in (glowstone)
/claim delete                      Delete the claim you stand in (blocks returned)    (also: /unclaim)
/claim deleteall                   Delete all your claims
/claim trust <player>              Let a player build in your claims
/claim untrust <player>            Remove a trusted player
/claim trustlist                   List trusted players

HOW TO CLAIM LAND
-----------------
1. /claim tool  (or tap "Get claim tool" in the claims menu)
2. Right-click one corner with the golden shovel.
3. Right-click the opposite corner. The claim costs 1 claim block per ground block.
4. Glowstone borders show for a few seconds. Sneak + right-click cancels a selection.
5. Right-click inside an existing claim with the shovel to see its owner and size.

Everyone starts with 300 claim blocks and earns 100 per hour of active (non-AFK) play.

WARPS
-----
/warp                              Open the server warps menu          (alias: /warps)
/warp <name>                       Teleport to a server warp (made by admins)
/pwarp                             Open the player warps menu          (aliases: /pw, /playerwarp)
/pwarp <name>                      Teleport to a player's warp
/pwarp set <name>                  Make (or move) your own warp here. Costs rupees to make a new one.
/pwarp delete <name>               Delete your warp
/pwarp list                        List your warps and your limit
/pwarp info <name>                 Owner and visits of a warp
  Every warp is safety-checked before teleporting. Unsafe warps are refused.

CLAIM BLOCKS: BUY AND SEND
--------------------------
/claim buy [amount]                Buy claim blocks with rupees (needs the economy plugin)
/claim send <player> <amount>      Give unused claim blocks to another player
/trapped                           Escape from inside someone else's claim   (alias: /stuck)

OTHER
-----
/arka version                      Plugin version
